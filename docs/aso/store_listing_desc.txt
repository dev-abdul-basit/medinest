Medinest – Pill Reminder

Short Desc:
Never miss your pills. Smart reminders, personal journals, and family support.

Full Desc:
Never Miss Your Medicine Again

Medinest is a simple and reliable pill reminder app designed to help you take your medications on time, keep personal journals or notes, and manage medication routines for yourself or your family — all in one place.

Whether you are managing daily medicines or supporting loved ones, Medinest keeps everything clear, organized, and stress-free.

Key Features

🔔 Smart Medicine Reminders
Add medicines with dosage, time, and frequency.
Set daily or custom schedules and receive timely notifications so you never miss a dose.

🧾 Medicine History
Maintain a clear record of when medications are taken.
Review past reminders to stay consistent and organized.

📓 Personal Journals & Notes
Write personal notes or journals alongside medications or independently.
No fixed templates — use notes in a way that fits your routine.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Member Support
Add and manage medications for family members separately.
Useful for caregivers managing multiple medication schedules.

👨‍⚕️ Doctor Management
Save doctor details and associate them with medications.
Easily remember prescriptions and references when needed.
